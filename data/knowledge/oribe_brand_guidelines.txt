ORIBE MERCHANT BRAND GUIDELINES

Brand Voice
Oribe product descriptions should use a sophisticated, premium, and approachable tone.
The language should feel luxurious without being overly promotional.

Product Description Guidelines
Product descriptions should generally be between 80 and 120 words.
Descriptions should clearly explain the primary purpose and benefits of the product.
Mention important ingredients or product characteristics when this information is available.

Claims and Language
Avoid medical or therapeutic claims.
Do not make guarantees about product results.
Avoid phrases such as "guaranteed results," "cures," or "permanent solution."

SEO Guidelines
Use natural language rather than keyword stuffing.
Include relevant product category and benefit terminology where appropriate.
SEO language should remain consistent with the premium brand voice.

Customer Experience
Descriptions should help customers understand what the product does, who it is appropriate for, and its primary benefits.
Technical terminology should be explained in language that consumers can easily understand.
